## Approved claim 1 – Primary endpoint (hero claim)

**Stat:** 38%

**Approved wording:**
Allerveo reduced total nasal symptom scores by 38% at week 2, compared with 21% for placebo.

**Footnote:**
BREEZE-2 Phase 3 study (ALV-302) of 1,248 patients; p<0.001.

---

## Approved claim 2 – Symptom coverage

**Stat:** 1 daily dose

**Approved wording:**
One dose a day treats both seasonal and year-round nasal allergy symptoms.

---

## Approved claim 3 – Tolerability

**Stat:** 2.1%

**Approved wording:**
In clinical trials, drowsiness was reported by 2.1% of patients taking Allerveo, compared with 1.8% taking placebo.

**Footnote:**
BREEZE-2 Phase 3 study (ALV-302) safety population.
