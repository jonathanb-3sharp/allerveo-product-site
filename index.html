Allerveo is a once-daily prescription treatment from Caldova designed for adults living with seasonal and year-round nasal allergy symptoms, including congestion, sneezing, runny nose and itchy nose.

What Allerveo showed in clinical trials

About the BREEZE-2 study

BREEZE-2 (study ALV-302) was a Phase 3 clinical trial of 1,248 patients with nasal allergy symptoms. Patients took either Allerveo or placebo once a day, and their total nasal symptom scores were measured over time.

Important Safety Information

Allerveo may cause drowsiness in some people. Do not drive or operate machinery until you know how Allerveo affects you. Tell your healthcare provider about all the medicines you take and any medical conditions you have.
